
A Key to Features in the Sample Grammar

2-OBJ-INF-OBJ: verbs like "persuade" that take 2 objects, one an Infinitive
ggS: phrase like "fifty seven"
AUXVERB: auxiliary verb
BAD: marks a constituent that Is ungrammatical
BIGNUM+: see the number grammar
BIGNUMG: "bignumber group"; don't ask, you don't want to know
COMP: complement markers like "that" and "for"
COMP-NP: NP node that dominates a complement S
COMP-OBJ: verb that takes some sort of complement as object
COMP-S: S which serves as a complement (oops! this is extrinsic.)
COMPLEX-NOUN: things like "Jon Jones", "April 5th"; treated like complex nouns
COMPLEX-NOUN-NP: a patch; an NP which is treated as a "complex noun"
COPULA:  copular auxiliary
DATE:  phrase which is a date
DECL:  declarative S
DEF:  definite, of either an article or an NP
DELTA:  unbound trace; "controlled" lexically
DET:  determiner
DETQ:  quantifier like "many" that can follow a determiner
EN:  verb with an "en" ending, no matter how spelled
EXISTENTIAL:  existential S, l.e. "there are...."
FINALPUNC:  final punctuation mark
FUTURE:  future tense
GENERAL-ORD:  ordinal like "next"
HOUR:  phrase like "2: 30"
HUNDRED+:  see the number grammar
IMPER:  imperative
INDEF:  indefinite, of either an article or an NP
INF:  infinitive verb form
INF-OBJ:  verb that can take an infinitive complement as object
INF-S:  infinitive S
ING:  verb with an "ing" ending, no matter how spelled
LISTNUM:  see the number grammar
MAJOR:  major S
MASSN:  mass noun
MASSNP:  NP whose head is a mass noun
MEASURE:  measure phrase like "ten foot"
MODAL:  ambiguous; either a modal or an aux with an attached modal
NIP:  noun 1st person
N2P:  noun, 2nd person
N3P:  noun 3rd person, of nouns, and dets (e.g. the), etc.
NAME:  name; e.g. "John"
NBAR:  N' node
NEXT-TIME-NP:  a hack; you don't want to know
NGSTART:  anything that could start an NP.
NO-SUBJ:  verbs that have delta subjects, realized as "it" sometimes, like "seems"
NOT-MODIFIABLE:  np which can take no restrictive modifiers
NP:  noun phrase
NP-PREPOSED:  S with an NP preposed into subject position
NP-QUEST:  question in which the np is fronted
NP-UTTERANCE:  utterance consisting only of an NP
NPL:  noun plural, of either nouns, or quantifiers like "some"
NS:  noun singular, of either nouns, or dets like "a", etc.
NUM:  number
NUMQP:  quantifier phrase that dominates a number
OBJ-BINDS-DELTA:   verb whose object binds the delta subj of an embedded clause
ONES:   word like "two", "five", etc.
ORDQP:   quantifier phrase that dominates an ordinal
PART:   participle
PASSIVE:   passive verb
PAST: past tense
PERF: perfective
POSS-DET: possessive determiner
POSS-NP: NP with a possessive determiner
PP-QUEST: question in which the pp is fronted
PP-UTTERANCE: utterance consisting only of a PP
PRED-VERB: anything which can follow a copula and Introduce a predicate phrase
PREDP: predicate phrase; follows a copula
PRES: present tense
PROG: progressive
PRON-NP: NP that dominates a pronoun
PROP-NP: NP whose head is a proper noun
PROPN-NP: NP that dominates a proper noun
PROPNOUN: proper noun
PSEUDOPROPNOUN: noun like "tomorrow" that behaves as If it were a proper noun
QP: quantifier phrase
QUANT: quantifier
QUANT-NP: NP consisting only of a quantifier, like "three"
QUEST: any kind of question
RELATIVE: relative clause
RELPRON: relative pronoun
SEC: secondary, l.e. a non-major, S
SLIGHTLY-BAD: slightly unacceptable constituent
SUBJ-BINDS-DELTA: verb whose subject binds the delta subj of an embedded clause
SUBJ-LESS-INF-OBJ: verbs like "want"
TENS: word like "ten", "twenty", etc.
THAT-OBJ: verb that takes a tensed complement
THAT-S: embedded finite complement
TIME: time word or phrase
TNSLESS: tenseless verb
TO-LESS-INF-OBJ: verb that takes infinitives, but w/o "to be", e.g. "John seemed happy."
TO-BE-LESS-INF-OBJ: verb like "help" that takes Infinitives but w/o "to", e.g. "1 helped him do it."
TRACE: np which is a trace
UTILIZED: WH-comp whose corresponding "gap" has been found
V+13S: this verb agrees with a 1st or 3rd person singular noun
V-3S: this verb agrees with any noun unless It Is 3rd singular
V1S: this verb agrees only with a 1st sing noun - "am"
V3S: this verb matches a 3rd singular noun
VERY-BAD: very unacceptable constituent
VPL+2S: this verb agrees with any plural or a 2nd person sing. noun
VSPL: this verb agrees with any noun, both singular and plural
WH: anything with a wh marker, either a det or an np with such a det
WHCOMP: any sort of wh phrase phrase
YEAR: which is a year
YNQUEST: yes/no question
